# Mission 19 OCR evidence boundary and safety checkpoint

Status: PARTIAL. Fail-first tests in `ocr-malformed-before.txt` demonstrate that malformed or unscored text-bearing Tesseract word rows could be silently dropped while other words were published. Adapter v12 rejects the whole page for invalid word evidence, incomplete word rows, or duplicate column headers. The PDF ingestion regression verifies no partial text is published on such failure. Parent and empty word rows remain non-evidence. This safety change does not improve or certify recognition quality, containment, or document deadlines.

The frozen corpus SHA-256 is `31aca80b4323fe470d34705e36038296ef620adb7525373f5196c9f561b6735d`; the corpus, labels and gate thresholds were not changed. Local diagnostic emulation used tesserocr 2.13.0 with Tesseract 5.5.4 and the pinned English and Persian model hashes; it is not the actual deployment image or CLI gate. The v11 baseline and v12 safety run each completed all 64 synthetic cases and 96 recognition passes and both **failed**: 311/384 critical fields, 1638/15305 character errors and 360/2328 word errors. All compared case metrics, thresholds, and corpus hash were identical between runs. See the machine-readable synthetic reports in this directory. Persian and table strata remain below mandatory thresholds; these synthetic outcomes do not constitute field evidence.

A one-case fixture-coordinate value-column experiment recovered six expected values with a mixed English/Persian model, but is exploratory only: there is no general detector, validated row association, corpus-wide result or deployed implementation. It was not adopted as a product or scorer change.

Focused regression log `ocr-v12-final.txt` records the ingestion and integration tests, including the deliberate PostgreSQL-only skip. Exact-SHA deployment-image CI remains mandatory and is not implied by this result. Process-group escape through `setsid`, Windows containment, and a whole-document deadline remain unresolved.
