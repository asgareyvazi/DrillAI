## 3. Detailed Domain Audit Analysis

### 3.1 Domain 3.1 — Tenant Boundaries, RBAC & Hierarchy Scope Resolution
- **Confirmed & Closed (`F-CP16-02`, `F-CP16-03`)**:
  - `GET /api/v1/wells/{well_id}/audit` (`backend/src/drillai/api/routers/twin.py:309-355`) previously queried `EngineRun`, `WorkflowRun`, `ToolCall`, `LlmCall`, and `NodeRun` by `well_id` alone without calling `_require_well` or filtering by `org_id == auth.org_id`. When Org A requested `/api/v1/wells/wel_beta/audit`, the endpoint returned `200 OK` containing Org B's workflow runs. Fixed by enforcing `_require_well(session, well_id, auth.org_id)` and `org_id == auth.org_id` filters across all five tables, and adding `_require_well` to `list_snapshots`, `twin_as_of`, `twin_changes`, `well_engine_runs`, and `well_recommendations`.
  - `TwinService.compare_snapshots` (`twin/service.py:381-405`) now filters both `TwinSnapshot` and `TwinAspect` by `org_id == self.org_id` and raises `NotFound` (`404`) when either snapshot is missing or belongs to another organization.
  - `TwinService.snapshot` (`twin/service.py:258-265`) now validates `scenario_id` and `parent_snapshot_id` against `org_id == self.org_id` and `well_id == well_id`.
  - `TwinService.write_aspect`, `WorkflowService.create`, `WorkflowService.start_run`, `GET /api/v1/wells/{well_id}/context`, and `POST /api/v1/registry/engines/{engine_key}/run` now validate nested hierarchy IDs (`project_id`, `well_id`, `wellbore_id`, `section_id`, `operation_id`) against the caller's tenant via `DocumentScopeResolver` / explicit tenant joins.
  - `WorkflowService.get_version` and `WorkflowService.history` now call `await self._workflow(workflow_id)` unconditionally and filter `WorkflowVersion.org_id == self.org_id`.

### 3.2 Domain 3.2 — Connector Security, SSRF Resistance & Async Event-Loop Blocking Hypothesis
- **Hypothesis Investigation (`F-CP16-01`)**:
  - **Hypothesis**: Synchronous `validate_connector_endpoint()` / `resolve_and_pin_endpoint()` invoked from `async def` worker and service methods blocks the asyncio event loop during slow DNS resolution.
  - **Root Cause Found**:
    1. `ConnectorWorker.execute_claimed_poll` (`worker.py:381`) and `ConnectorService.create`, `update`, `preview`, and `replay` (`connectors.py:3719, 3858, 4617, 4759`) called synchronous `validate_connector_endpoint(...)` directly inside `async def` functions.
    2. Even inside `resolve_and_pin_endpoint_async`, when `require_dns_resolution=False` and `_custom_dns_resolver is None`, the async function skipped its non-blocking `asyncio.wrap_future(fut)` path and fell through to synchronous `resolve_and_pin_endpoint()`, which acquired `_DNS_SEMAPHORE.acquire(timeout=0.2)` and blocked on `fut_be.result(timeout=1.0)` on the event loop thread.
  - **Quantified Impact**: Under a simulated 180ms DNS lookup (`time.sleep(0.18)` in `getaddrinfo`), the event-loop ticker (`10ms` interval) experienced **`max_tick_lag_s = 0.181s`** (complete event-loop stall for the duration of the DNS lookup).
  - **Resolution**: Added `validate_connector_endpoint_async()`, updated `resolve_and_pin_endpoint_async()` so both `require_dns_resolution=True` and `require_dns_resolution=False` await `asyncio.wait_for(asyncio.wrap_future(fut), timeout=...)` on the bounded DNS executor and pass `_pre_resolved_ips=resolved_strs` to `resolve_and_pin_endpoint()`, and switched all async callers in `ConnectorService` and `ConnectorWorker` to `await validate_connector_endpoint_async(...)`. Event-loop tick lag during 180ms DNS lookups dropped from **`181ms` to `< 3ms`**, while concurrent coroutines complete `>= 10` ticks during each DNS wait.

### 3.4 Domain 3.4 — Engineering Engines & Physical Data Correctness
- **Confirmed & Closed (`F-CP16-05`, `F-CP16-06`)**:
  - Fixed the `"m2"` symbol collision in `backend/src/drillai/units/registry.py` so `get_unit("m2")` is `Dimension.AREA`, `get_unit("m2-perm")` is `Dimension.PERMEABILITY`, cross-dimension duplicate symbol registration raises `UnitError`, and `convert()` supports SI `"m2"` conversions for both `AREA` (`in2 <-> m2`) and `PERMEABILITY` (`mD <-> m2`, `mD <-> m2-perm`).
  - Fixed `tubulars.api_5c3` (`backend/src/drillai/engines/tubulars.py`) so `_triaxial` receives the effective external pressure (`external`, including hydrostatic `mud_weight_si * GRAVITY * to_depth_si`) and returns `None` instead of non-JSON `float("inf")` when equivalent stress is zero.
- **Independent Reference Benchmark Verification (`test_f_cp16_06_independent_physics_reference_benchmarks`)**:
  1. **Trajectory Minimum Curvature (`trajectory.minimum_curvature`)**: Benchmarked against SPE-84246 / Bourgoyne et al. *Applied Drilling Engineering* analytical circular-arc equations ($\Delta \text{MD} = 300\,\text{m}$, $I_1 = 10^\circ \to I_2 = 25^\circ$, $A_1 = 30^\circ \to A_2 = 60^\circ$); matches analytical $\text{TVD}$, $\text{North}$, $\text{East}$, and $\text{DLS}$ within `< 1e-12` relative tolerance.
  2. **API TR 5C3 Tubular Ratings (`tubulars.api_5c3`)**: Benchmarked against API TR 5C3 (2018) Barlow internal yield pressure ($P_{iY} = 2 \cdot 0.875 \cdot f_y \cdot t / D$), 4-domain collapse ($D/t = 20.39 \implies$ plastic collapse domain), and Lamé thick-wall inner-bore von Mises stress under hydrostatic mud load; matches within `< 1e-9` relative tolerance.
  3. **Bingham Plastic / Power-Law Laminar Hydraulics (`hydraulics.laminar`)**: Benchmarked against Bourgoyne et al. pipe and slot-annulus Hagen-Poiseuille pressure loss, ECD ($\rho_{\text{eff}} = \rho_m + \Delta P_{\text{ann}} / (g \cdot \text{TVD})$), and jet nozzle Bernoulli bit pressure drop ($\Delta P_{\text{bit}} = \rho_m v_n^2 / (2 C_d^2)$); matches within `< 1e-9` relative tolerance.
  4. **Well Control Wait-and-Weight Kill Sheet (`well_control.kill_sheet`)**: Benchmarked against IWCF / IADC standard hydrostatic equations ($\text{KMW} = \rho_m + \text{SIDPP} / (g \cdot \text{TVD})$, $\text{ICP} = P_{\text{scr}} + \text{SIDPP}$, $\text{FCP} = P_{\text{scr}} \cdot \text{KMW}/\rho_m$, $\text{MAASP} = (\rho_{\text{LOT}} - \text{KMW}) g \cdot \text{TVD}_{\text{shoe}}$); matches within `< 1e-12` relative tolerance.
  5. **Teale (1965) Mechanical Specific Energy (`drilling.mse`)**: Benchmarked against Teale's *Int. J. Rock Mech. Min. Sci.* Vol. 2 equation ($\text{MSE} = \frac{4 W}{\pi D^2} + \frac{480 N T}{D^2 \cdot \text{ROP}}$); matches axial, torsional, and total MSE within `< 1e-12` relative tolerance.
