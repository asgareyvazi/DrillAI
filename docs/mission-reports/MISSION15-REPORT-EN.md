Database-created sessions now carry an owning database reference, encrypted connector and notification secret resolution uses that database's explicit settings, and unscoped encrypted-secret resolution fails closed. Connector worker defaults use its database's settings. A real notification-worker lifecycle with a synthetic local adapter and concurrent two-application secret resolution were tested.
